Help

FAQ

Who can participate?

Anyone. The run is open to students, NCC cadets, faculty, families and the general public. MAHE students can add their registration number, but it's optional.

How do I pay?

Only UPI is accepted: Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM or any bank UPI app. Scan the QR code or tap your app, pay, then enter the 12-digit UTR from your app.

Where do I find the UTR / transaction ID?

Open the payment in your UPI app's history. Google Pay shows "UPI transaction ID", PhonePe shows "UTR", Paytm shows "UPI Ref No." It's always 12 digits.

When will my registration be confirmed?

You get a registration ID right away. Organisers match your UTR with the received payments and email you once it's verified, usually within 24–48 hours.

Money was deducted but the form didn't submit. What now?

Fill in the form again with the same UTR. If it says the UTR is already used, your registration went through. Otherwise, contact the coordinators below with your payment screenshot.

Is the fee refundable?

Entry fees are non-refundable and non-transferable unless the event is cancelled by the organisers.
